Justlife Healthcare Teleconsultation

Privacy notice — Teleconsultation booking

Please have this text reviewed by Justlife's legal / data-protection team before going live.

What we collect

When you book a teleconsultation we collect your name, email address, mobile number, your Justlife booking reference, your preferred contact method (phone or WhatsApp), any notes you add, and any lab results or documents you choose to upload. During the consultation the doctor records clinical notes and any services they recommend.

Why we use it

Only to arrange and deliver your teleconsultation: to let the doctor contact you in your chosen time window, to send you confirmation, reminder and change emails, to let you reschedule or cancel, and to follow up on services you agree to book.

Who can see it

Your information is visible only to authorised Justlife Healthcare staff and to the doctor assigned to your appointment. Uploaded documents are stored privately and are never published on the internet. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and consultation records for as long as required by UAE healthcare record-keeping rules, and then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or (where the law allows) to delete it. Contact us at healthcare@justlife.com.
